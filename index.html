Who we are

Our story

Yayasan Pemangkin's journey began in 2004, responding to the Aceh tsunami and going on to support thousands of students, families and volunteers across Malaysia. YP Tech is the foundation's dedicated fintech subsidiary, created to bring that impact to the financing industry.

We turn a twenty-year heritage of trust and accountability into enterprise-grade financial technology. Our platforms digitise financing from application to repayment, speed up approvals and prove where every ringgit goes, for Pemangkin's own programmes and for partner financiers.

What we deliver

Fintech solutions for the financing industry

Modular, API-first services for banks, development financial institutions, cooperatives and financing companies, deployed on their own or as one financing operating layer.

Digital Financing & Loan Origination

An end-to-end digital financing journey, from online application to automated underwriting, e-agreement and same-day disbursement.

  • Web & mobile financing applications
  • Automated underwriting & decisioning
  • e-Agreement, e-stamping & instant disbursement

Cooperative & SME Financing

A configurable financing product engine for cooperatives, SMEs and community lenders, with flexible terms, rate calculation and full audit trails.

  • Personal, SME & asset financing products
  • Cooperative member-financing modules
  • Microfinancing for micro-entrepreneurs

Financing Management & Collections

Servicing for the full financing lifecycle, with automated repayments, early-warning alerts and digital collections workflows.

  • Auto-debit, DuitNow & FPX repayments
  • Early-warning delinquency prediction
  • Restructuring & collections case management

Blockchain Financing Infrastructure

Permissioned ledgers that keep an immutable record of every financing agreement, disbursement and repayment for financiers, auditors and regulators.

  • Tamper-proof financing records
  • Smart-contract disbursement rules
  • Tokenised & asset-backed financing
